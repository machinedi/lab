Not a product for sale. A personal lab note.

A trainer builds a structured plan. A trainee sees that plan in a dedicated view: the session in front of them, the sets, the history. A model can help the trainer notice drift and suggest an adjustment. It does not rewrite the block on its own.

The split is the point. The trainer owns the plan. The trainee owns the log. The model proposes. A person still has to accept.
