Trainer OS is a hobby. A trainer builds a structured plan. A trainee sees that plan in a dedicated app: the session in front of them, the sets, the performance history. As the plan progresses, AI helps the trainer see how people are actually doing and suggest adjustments that respect the structure, not vibes from a chat.

The split matters. The trainer owns the plan. The trainee owns the log. The model does not get to rewrite the block because a session felt poetic. It can surface drift (missed sessions, stalled lifts, pain flags) and propose a change the trainer still has to accept.

I am not pretending this is a launched company. There is no brand campaign, no pricing page, no "we are revolutionizing fitness." It is a trainer-athlete product I run as a home lab: enough structure that an agent can help without being allowed to invent the sport.
